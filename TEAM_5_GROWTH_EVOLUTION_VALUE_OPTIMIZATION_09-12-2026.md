## 3. Strategic reinvention (pipeline role)

Moving beyond basic maintenance, Team 5 drives **strategic reinvention**: redesign business processes around agentic capabilities—not only automate yesterday’s tickets.

In the forward pipeline, Team 5 sits **after** Governance so value and learning are scored on what actually shipped (or was vetoed). In the closed loop, Team 5 sits **before** the next Discovery cycle so Architects redesign from evidence.

| Pipeline stance | Team 5 contribution |
|-----------------|---------------------|
| **Forward** | Score ROI / soft benefits; brief CEO on whether the generation created value |
| **Closed loop** | World-model update + exploratory proposals → Team 1 modular blueprints |
| **Enterprise 2026** | Agents as mainstream ops → continuous reinvention with humans central |

## 4. Sub-roles

### 4.1 Metric Sentinel

**Mission:** Quantify whether the agentic system is creating real value.

Monitors:

* **Hard metrics:** ROI, time saved, cycle-time reduction, user adoption, revenue uplift, error reduction, deploy success rate, hotfix frequency.  
* **Soft benefits:** employee satisfaction, reduced burnout, clearer decision latency, trust in automation.  
* **Operational telemetry:** uptime, swarm/task outcomes, blackboard-derived health.

Produces: rolling snapshots, trend views, and alerts that Strategy Architect and Evolutionary Learner consume.

#### Metric Sentinel after Team 4 deployment

Once Team 4 records a technical **Go** (or material **Conditional Go**) and completes deploy stage ⑥—then **⑦ Notify Team 5** per [`TEAM_4_CICD_DEPLOYMENT_AND_GO_NO_GO_09-12-2026.md`](TEAM_4_CICD_DEPLOYMENT_AND_GO_NO_GO_09-12-2026.md)—Metric Sentinel attaches **post-deploy value context**. Scoring starts **after** governance approval; pre-gate drafts are not treated as shipped value.

| Track | What Sentinel measures post-Go | Notes |
|-------|--------------------------------|--------|
| **ROI** | Value vs cost of the shipped modular unit(s) | Formulas **not coded**; manual/CEO briefs until KPI catalog |
| **Cycle time** | Discovery→ship latency; hotfix MTTR; gate dwell time | Compare steady-state vs emergency path |
| **Adoption** | Use of the shipped module (routes, skill hits, MCP calls) | Attribute to **module id** where possible |
| **Errors** | Post-deploy error rate, rollback frequency, recurrence | Pair with Team 3 hotfix tags |
| **Soft benefits** | Satisfaction, burnout relief, decision latency, trust in automation | Inspectable claims; not vanity dashboards |

```text
Team 4 ⑤ Go/No-Go ──► ⑥ Deploy ──► ⑦ Notify Team 5
                                         │
                                         ▼
                              Metric Sentinel (post-deploy)
                                 ROI · cycle time · adoption · errors · soft benefits
                                         │
                                         ▼
                              Evolutionary Learner (+ Strategy Architect)
```

**Partial map → `weaver_logging_suite`:** today only operational telemetry is harvestable via [`weaver_logging_suite.py`](weaver_logging_suite.py) / [`WEAVER_ENGINE_AUTOMATED_LOGGING_SUITE_09-12-2026.md`](WEAVER_ENGINE_AUTOMATED_LOGGING_SUITE_09-12-2026.md) (`performance_metrics.json`, `uptime_dashboard.txt`, blackboard-derived health). That substrate can support **errors / uptime / task outcomes** slices of post-deploy scoring; it does **not** compute ROI, soft benefits, adoption funnels, or CEO packs. Label any logging-only view as **partial Metric Sentinel**.

**Rules:**

* Do **not** score “value created” for **No-Go** ships (veto still feeds Learner as governance friction).  
* Rollbacks after Go are first-class negative signals (cycle time + errors).  
* Humans/CEO validate soft-benefit and ROI claims before investment pivots.  
* Full KPI + soft-benefits catalog (**v1.1.0** — mission, KPI table, soft measurement, peer roles, post-Go tracking, as-built vs not, CEO insights): [`TEAM_5_METRIC_SENTINEL_KPI_AND_SOFT_BENEFITS_09-12-2026.md`](TEAM_5_METRIC_SENTINEL_KPI_AND_SOFT_BENEFITS_09-12-2026.md).

**Mission:** Keep the product roadmap honest against markets and competitors.

Activities:

* Analyze competitor data and market trends (internet / research feeds).  
* Recommend new features, retirements, or pivots based on real-time evidence.  
* Prioritize Evolutionary Learner explorations that matter commercially.  
* Brief the human CEO with **options and trade-offs**—not a single silent decision.

### 4.3 Evolutionary Learner

**Mission:** Turn operational outcomes into a better world model and better blueprints.

Activities:

* Ingest Team 3 (Support) and Team 4 (Gatekeepers) outcomes as training / evaluation signals.  
* Update beliefs about fragile modules, failed blueprint assumptions, repeated governance blocks, ROI-correlated patterns.  
* Propose exploratory actions (refactors, new MCP/hook surfaces, retirement of brittle units, process redesigns).  
* Hand **human-accepted** explorations to Team 1 Architects for new modular blueprints.

## 5. CEO partnership & humans central

While Team 5 functions with high autonomy in drafting insights, **humans remain central**.

| Actor | Owns |
|-------|------|
| **Metric Sentinel / Strategy Architect / Evolutionary Learner** | Evidence packs, ranked options, world-model drafts |
| **Humans (ops / eng)** | Validate metrics that might game the system; request alternate explorations |
| **Human CEO / executive** | Final authority on ethics, irreversible risk, strategy pivots, investment priority among proposals |

### 5.1 Non-negotiable

* Team 5 **advises**; it does not silently pivot the company.  
* Exploratory actions require human acceptance before Architects rewrite the product spine.  
* Soft-benefit and ROI claims must be inspectable.  
* Technical Go from Team 4 does not replace CEO ethics/strategy halt.

### 5.2 Collaboration pattern

1. Agents prepare options with evidence (metrics + market + incident / veto history).  
2. Humans choose among options or request another exploration.  
3. Chosen direction becomes Team 1 blueprint work → Builders → Support → Gatekeepers → Team 5 again.

**Deep-dive:** [`TEAM_5_STRATEGIC_PARTNER_TO_HUMAN_CEO_09-12-2026.md`](TEAM_5_STRATEGIC_PARTNER_TO_HUMAN_CEO_09-12-2026.md) · protocol: [`MASTER_ORCHESTRATOR_TEAM5_CEO_HANDOFF_ADDENDUM_09-12-2026.md`](MASTER_ORCHESTRATOR_TEAM5_CEO_HANDOFF_ADDENDUM_09-12-2026.md).

## 6. Evolutionary feedback → Team 1 (numbered loop)

Parent framework §5 is authoritative. Summary for Team 5 operators:

1. **Collect outcomes** — Support hotfixes/incidents + Gatekeeper deploy/compliance/veto records.  
2. **Enrich with value context** — Metric Sentinel attaches ROI, adoption, error, soft-benefit trends.  
3. **Update world model** — Evolutionary Learner revises fragility / contract / governance beliefs.  
4. **Propose explorations** — Candidate refactors, new MCP/hook surfaces, retirements, process redesigns.  
5. **Prioritize commercially** — Strategy Architect ranks with market evidence and CEO priorities.  
6. **Human / CEO gate** — Accept, reject, or defer.  
7. **Re-architect** — Team 1 turns accepted proposals into modular blueprints.  
8. **Rebuild** — Team 2 implements next-generation modules.  
9. **Operate & govern** — Team 3 and Team 4 run against the new generation.  
10. **Close the loop** — Outcomes return to step 1.

```text
Team 3 + Team 4 outcomes ──► Evolutionary Learner
                                    │
                                    ▼
                            world-model update
                                    │
                                    ▼
              exploratory proposals (+ Strategy Architect)
                                    │
                                    ▼
                             CEO / human gate
                                    │
                                    ▼
                          Team 1 Architects (new blueprints)
```

**As-built:** this loop is **documented only**—not automated in Weaver.

## 7. Modular architecture & enterprise context (2026)

### 7.1 Modular architecture (Team 5 implications)

* Learner proposals must target **replaceable units** (MCP / hook / skill / extension), not “rewrite the monolith.”  
* Metric Sentinel scores should attribute value/pain to **module ids** where possible.  
* Portable packaging: proposals and metrics travel with the project tree; no machine-absolute product paths.

### 7.2 Enterprise (2026)

* Autonomous agents as mainstream components require continuous **value proof** and **governed reinvention**.  
* Team 5 + CEO partnership is mandatory once agents ship into production paths.  
* Humans remain central for ethics, strategy confirmation, and investment priority.
