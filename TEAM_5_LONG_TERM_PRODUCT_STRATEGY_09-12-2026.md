## 5. CEO partnership & human confirmation

Team 5 **advises**; humans remain central.

| Actor | Owns |
|-------|------|
| Metric Sentinel / Strategy Architect / Evolutionary Learner | Evidence packs, ranked options, world-model drafts |
| Humans (ops / eng) | Validate metrics that might game the system; request alternate explorations |
| **Human CEO / executive** | Final authority on ethics, irreversible risk, strategy pivots, investment priority |

### 5.1 Human confirmation (non-negotiable)

1. Agents prepare options with evidence (metrics + market + incident / veto history).  
2. Humans/CEO **accept, reject, or defer**—or request another exploration.  
3. Only **accepted** directions become Team 1 blueprint work.  
4. Technical Go from Team 4 does **not** replace CEO ethics/strategy halt.  
5. Soft-benefit and ROI claims must be inspectable.

CEO partnership deep-dive: [`TEAM_5_STRATEGIC_PARTNER_TO_HUMAN_CEO_09-12-2026.md`](TEAM_5_STRATEGIC_PARTNER_TO_HUMAN_CEO_09-12-2026.md).
