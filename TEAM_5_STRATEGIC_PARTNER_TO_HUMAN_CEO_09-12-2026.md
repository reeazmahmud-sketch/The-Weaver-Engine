## 1. Partnership thesis

| Principle | Meaning |
|-----------|---------|
| **Advise, do not pivot** | Team 5 drafts options; CEO decides strategy |
| **Data-centric** | Every material recommendation carries inspectable evidence |
| **Humans central** | Ethics, irreversible risk, and investment priority stay with humans |
| **Closed loop after CEO gate** | Only **Accepted** explorations become Team 1 Discovery intake |
| **Two different gates** | Team 4 = technical ship; CEO + Team 5 = value / strategy |

### 1.1 One-line role

**Team 5 prepares the evidence pack; the human CEO chooses among options or requests another exploration.**

### 1.2 Explicit non-goals for this partnership

* Do **not** silently change product direction.  
* Do **not** treat Team 4 **Go** as executive or ROI approval.  
* Do **not** bury soft-benefit or ROI claims behind vanity dashboards.  
* Do **not** forward Unaccepted explorations to Team 1.  
* Do **not** claim CEO packs or strategy agents are coded in Weaver.

## 2. Data-centric insights Team 5 owes the CEO

Team 5’s briefing stack has three evidence lanes. All three may appear in one CEO pack when the decision is strategic.

### 2.1 Metric Sentinel — hard KPIs + soft benefits

| Lane | CEO question answered |
|------|------------------------|
| **Hard KPIs** | Did the ship create measurable economic/operational value (ROI, cycle time, adoption, time saved, revenue uplift, error reduction)? |
| **Soft benefits** | Did humans get better outcomes (satisfaction, reduced burnout, clearer decision latency, trust in automation)? |
| **Ops telemetry** | Is the substrate healthy enough to trust the other lanes (uptime, task outcomes, module-attributed errors)? |

Catalog: [`TEAM_5_METRIC_SENTINEL_KPI_AND_SOFT_BENEFITS_09-12-2026.md`](TEAM_5_METRIC_SENTINEL_KPI_AND_SOFT_BENEFITS_09-12-2026.md).  
**As-built:** `weaver_logging_suite` = **partial** hard/ops substrate only—not full CEO packs.

**Rule for CEO packs:** label every soft-benefit claim with method (survey / proxy / interview) and window. Unlabeled soft claims are invalid.

### 2.2 Workflow / business-process redesign

Strategic reinvention means redesigning workflows around agentic capabilities—not automating yesterday’s tickets one-for-one.

CEO pack should state:

* Which process is proposed for redesign  
* Which modular units (MCP / hook / skill / extension) would carry the change  
* Expected hard + soft value deltas  
* Human roles that remain after redesign  
* Risks (ethics, irreversible process lock-in, gaming metrics)

Detail: [`TEAM_5_BUSINESS_PROCESS_REDESIGN_AGENTIC_CAPABILITIES_09-12-2026.md`](TEAM_5_BUSINESS_PROCESS_REDESIGN_AGENTIC_CAPABILITIES_09-12-2026.md).

## 3. Human-in-the-loop vs Team 4 Go/No-Go

Team 4 and the CEO answer **different** questions on the same generation of work:

| Question | Owner | Horizon |
|----------|-------|---------|
| “Is this unit safe, compliant, and contract-sound to ship?” | **Team 4** technical Go/No-Go | Pre-deploy / deploy |
| “Did what we shipped (or vetoed) create ongoing value worth continuing / reinventing?” | **Team 5** Metric Sentinel + strategy cycle | Post-Go tracking |
| “Should we pivot product direction, ethics posture, or investment priority?” | **Human CEO** | Strategy / irreversible risk |

```text
Team 4 Go / No-Go / Conditional Go
         │
         ▼
   Deploy or veto recorded ──► Metric Sentinel window
         │
         ▼
Strategy Architect + Evolutionary Learner ──► CEO options pack
         │
         ▼
   CEO Accept / Reject / Defer / Halt
         │
         └── only Accepted ──► Team 1 Architects
```

### 3.1 Implications (non-negotiable)

* Technical **Go** ≠ ROI proof ≠ CEO strategy approval.  
* CEO may **halt** after technical Go for ethics or strategy.  
* Team 5 does **not** override Team 4 **No-Go**.  
* **No-Go**, rollback, and Conditional constraints still feed Evolutionary Learner as signals.  
* Until CI/CD exists, Admin holds Team 4 gates manually; CEO packs remain doc/manual with partial logging substrate.

## 4. Communication & handoff notes to the CEO

Protocol detail lives in the addendum (do not duplicate the full Master Orchestrator). Summary for operators:

### 4.1 When to brief the CEO

| Trigger | Typical pack contents |
|---------|----------------------|
| Post–Team 4 deploy / notify (stage ⑦) | Value window open; early hard/ops signals; soft-benefit plan |
| Material veto / rollback | Fragility signal + recommended exploration options |
| Strategy / market shift | Ranked pivots with trade-offs |
| Recurring hotfix pattern | Re-blueprint ask (not another silent patch) |
| Ethics / irreversible risk | Escalation stop; options A/B; recommended human question |

### 4.2 CEO decision vocabulary (Team 5 gate)

| Decision | Effect |
|----------|--------|
| **Accept** | Item may enter Team 1 Discovery intake |
| **Reject** | Recorded; may still update Learner world model as “rejected path” |
| **Defer** | Parked with revisit condition; not Discovery intake |
| **Halt** | Freeze related T5→T1 Accepted queue / ship path for ethics or strategy |

### 4.3 Minimum CEO pack fields

1. Decision asked (Accept / Reject / Defer / Halt)  
2. Evidence summary (Metric Sentinel hard + soft + ops; label **partial** when logging-only)  
3. Options (≥2 when strategy pivot; each with modular target shape)  
4. Trade-offs (value, risk, time, blast radius)  
5. Explicit contrast: Team 4 technical status vs this CEO ask  
6. Recommended next team action if Accepted (usually Team 1 blueprint)

Full envelope: [`MASTER_ORCHESTRATOR_TEAM5_CEO_HANDOFF_ADDENDUM_09-12-2026.md`](MASTER_ORCHESTRATOR_TEAM5_CEO_HANDOFF_ADDENDUM_09-12-2026.md).

### 4.4 Collaboration pattern (standing)

1. Agents prepare options with evidence (metrics + market + incident / veto history).  
2. Humans / CEO choose among options or request another exploration.  
3. Chosen direction becomes Team 1 blueprint work → Builders → Support → Gatekeepers → Team 5 again.
