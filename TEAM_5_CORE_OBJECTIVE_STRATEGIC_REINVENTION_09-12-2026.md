## 1. Core objective

| Dimension | Team 5 owns |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Strategic reinvention** | Redesign business processes around agentic capabilities—**beyond maintenance**; not only automate yesterday’s tickets |
| **Ongoing value** | Continuously score whether shipped agentic work creates hard ROI **and** soft human benefits |
| **Full autonomy leverage** | Use the five-team autonomous loop (draft → ship → govern → learn → re-architect) at scale without babysitting every insight |
| **Learning loop** | Turn Support + Gatekeeper outcomes into world-model updates and exploratory proposals |
| **CEO partnership** | Brief the human CEO with inspectable options; never silent autonomous pivots |

### 1.1 One-line mission

**Prove value → advise strategy → learn → propose reinvention → (CEO gate) → re-architect via Team 1.**

### 1.2 Explicit non-goals

* Do **not** hold technical Go/No-Go (Team 4).  
* Do **not** author hotfixes (Team 3) or production modules (Team 2).  
* Do **not** replace Discovery (Team 1)—Team 5 **feeds** Architects.  
* Do **not** treat a green CI gate or vanity dashboard as value proof.  
* Do **not** claim the evolutionary loop or full Metric Sentinel is coded in Weaver.  
* Do **not** confuse high drafting autonomy with unsupervised strategy pivots.

## 2. Beyond maintenance — strategic reinvention

Maintenance keeps yesterday’s tickets green. **Strategic reinvention** asks whether agentic capabilities should **reshape the process** itself:

| Maintenance stance | Reinvention stance |
|--------------------|--------------------|
| Automate the same workflow faster | Redesign the workflow around agents as first-class operators |
| Close incidents | Learn from incidents into next-gen blueprints |
| Celebrate deploy success | Ask whether the ship created hard + soft value worth continuing |
| One-shot Discovery | Closed-loop Discovery fed by Evolutionary Learner evidence |

Enterprise 2026 context: agents as mainstream ops make continuous reinvention mandatory—with **humans central** for ethics and investment priority.

## 3. Ongoing value vs Team 4 Go/No-Go (alignment)

Team 4 and Team 5 answer **different questions** on the same ship:

| Question | Owner | Time horizon |
|----------|-------|--------------|
| “Is this unit safe, compliant, and contract-sound to ship?” | **Team 4** technical Go/No-Go | Pre-deploy / deploy |
| “Did what we shipped (or vetoed) create ongoing value worth continuing / reinventing?” | **Team 5** Value Optimization | Post-Go tracking + strategy cycles |

```text
Team 4 Go / No-Go / Conditional Go
         │
         ▼
   Deploy or veto recorded
         │
         ▼
Team 5 Metric Sentinel ──► ongoing value (hard + soft)
         │
         ▼
Strategy Architect + Evolutionary Learner ──► CEO options ──► Team 1
```

### 3.1 Implications

* A **technical Go** is necessary for production ship; it is **not** sufficient as ROI or soft-benefit proof.  
* A **No-Go** or **rollback** is still a Team 5 signal (fragility, contract debt, governance pattern)—value work continues on vetoed paths.  
* Soft benefits (satisfaction, burnout reduction) are **first-class** Team 5 concerns; Team 4 does not gate on them.  
* Until CI/CD exists, Admin holds Team 4 gates manually; Team 5 scoring remains doc/manual with partial logging substrate.

Detail: [`TEAM_4_GO_NO_GO_AUTHORITY_09-12-2026.md`](TEAM_4_GO_NO_GO_AUTHORITY_09-12-2026.md); KPI catalog: [`TEAM_5_METRIC_SENTINEL_KPI_AND_SOFT_BENEFITS_09-12-2026.md`](TEAM_5_METRIC_SENTINEL_KPI_AND_SOFT_BENEFITS_09-12-2026.md).

## 4. Full autonomy leverage

Strategic reinvention **leverages full autonomy** of the agentic lifecycle—without unsupervised company pivots:

| Leverage | Meaning |
|----------|---------|
| **Autonomous drafting** | Metric Sentinel / Strategy Architect / Evolutionary Learner prepare evidence packs and options without Admin typing every analysis |
| **Closed-loop operation** | Outcomes from Teams 3–4 continuously feed world-model updates and next Discovery (documented protocol until coded) |
| **Scale without babysitting** | Humans review ranked options and veto risky explorations; they do not re-run every metric harvest by hand |
| **Portable self-operation** | Proposals and metrics travel with the project tree; reinvention does not depend on one Mac’s absolute paths |

**Hard boundary:** full autonomy leverage = **high autonomy in sensing, scoring, and proposing**; **CEO partnership** retains exclusive authority on ethics, irreversible risk, and strategy confirmation. Autonomy without the CEO gate is not reinvention—it is silent drift.

## 6. CEO partnership (humans central)

| Actor | Owns |
|-------|------|
| Metric Sentinel / Strategy Architect / Evolutionary Learner | Evidence packs, ranked options, world-model drafts |
| Humans (ops / eng) | Validate metrics that might game the system; request alternate explorations |
| **Human CEO / executive** | Final authority on ethics, irreversible risk, strategy pivots, investment priority |

### 6.1 Non-negotiable

* Team 5 **advises**; it does not silently pivot the company.  
* Exploratory actions require human acceptance before Architects rewrite the product spine.  
* Soft-benefit and ROI claims must be inspectable.  
* Technical Go from Team 4 does not replace CEO ethics/strategy halt.
